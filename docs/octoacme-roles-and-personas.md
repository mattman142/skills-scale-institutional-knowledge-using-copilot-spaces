# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, design test plans, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Own the testing strategy and test plan for the project
- Design and maintain test cases for unit, integration, end-to-end, and security coverage
- Establish quality standards and acceptance criteria with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Developers
- Conduct manual QA and acceptance testing when needed
- Work with Developers to integrate automated testing into CI/CD pipelines
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Participate in release reviews and post-release validation

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, defect-free releases
- Reduce rework and production incidents through comprehensive testing
- Enable confident deployments with clear quality signals

### Typical Communication
- Planning meetings to define acceptance criteria and test approaches
- Sprint standups and QA status updates
- Release readiness reviews
- Incident triage and root cause analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria and validate outcomes
- Works with Developers to create testable stories and automate regression coverage
- Coordinates with Project Managers on test readiness, release timing, and quality status

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide solution design, technical decisions, and architecture quality across the project. They help the team balance delivery speed with long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architecture for the solution
- Review design proposals and identify trade-offs
- Manage technical risks, dependencies, and integration points
- Support Developers with implementation guidance and difficult technical decisions
- Ensure system design aligns with scalability, performance, and maintainability goals
- Collaborate on technical standards and patterns

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical risk and architectural debt
- Improve team confidence in design and implementation choices

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical planning sessions
- Code review feedback and implementation guidance
- Risk and dependency discussions during delivery syncs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on implementation choices and technical problem-solving
- Collaborates with Product Managers to balance scope, value, and feasibility
- Advises Project Managers on technical risks, dependencies, and schedule impact

---

## Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives act as the voice of business and functional stakeholders, ensuring their needs, constraints, and feedback are reflected in project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Consolidate stakeholder input and represent business priorities
- Review progress, scope changes, and decisions that affect stakeholders
- Clarify business constraints, operational impacts, and adoption concerns
- Help validate that delivered work meets organizational expectations
- Raise issues, requests, or risks from their stakeholder group

### Goals
- Keep stakeholder needs visible and aligned with delivery decisions
- Reduce misalignment between delivery teams and business groups
- Support informed decision-making across departments

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based status reviews
- Feedback sessions during planning and review
- Decision-making discussions when scope or priorities change

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine requirements and business priorities
- Coordinates with Project Managers on communications, dependencies, and stakeholder updates
- Engages with Developers and QA/Testing Leads during review and acceptance when needed

---

## Sponsors / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Sponsors / Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, resource support, and executive-level escalation for projects. They ensure alignment with organizational goals and have authority to make high-impact decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case
- Allocate budget and resources
- Serve as escalation point for critical blockers and decisions
- Track project health and outcomes against strategic objectives
- Communicate project status to senior leadership
- Remove organizational barriers and enable team execution

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and strategic alignment
- Remove obstacles at the organizational level
- Make timely, informed decisions on trade-offs and priorities

### Typical Communication
- Executive sponsorship reviews, monthly or milestone-based
- Escalation paths for critical decisions
- Post-project business impact reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on priority, outcomes, and business value
- Supports Project Managers with escalation and resource decisions
- Receives risk, status, and release information when issues need executive attention

---

## Security / Compliance Officers

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Officers ensure security and compliance requirements are considered throughout planning, delivery, and release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements relevant to the project
- Review risks, controls, and mitigations for delivery activities
- Advise on secure design, data handling, and release readiness
- Support incident response and security issue triage when needed
- Validate that processes and artifacts meet applicable policy or regulatory needs

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Ensure required controls are in place before release
- Help the team ship safely and responsibly

### Typical Communication
- Security and compliance reviews during planning and release
- Risk discussions during delivery and incident handling
- Ad hoc consultation when sensitive changes are introduced

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Technical Leads / Architects on secure design and technical controls
- Partners with Developers on implementation of security requirements
- Coordinates with QA/Testing Leads on security testing and validation
- Advises Project Managers on risk escalation and release constraints
