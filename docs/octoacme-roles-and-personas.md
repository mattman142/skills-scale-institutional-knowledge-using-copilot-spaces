# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, maintain test plans, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Own project-level testing strategy and quality gates
- Define and maintain test plans across unit, integration, and end-to-end coverage
- Partner with teams to clarify acceptance criteria and testability requirements
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations before release
- Participate in release readiness checks and post-release validation

### Goals
- Improve release quality and reduce escaped defects
- Create clear quality signals for go/no-go decisions
- Reduce rework through earlier defect detection

### Typical Communication
- Planning sessions for acceptance criteria and test approach
- Sprint QA status updates and defect triage
- Release readiness reviews and post-release findings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Aligns on testability, defect reproduction, and fix validation
- **Product Managers:** Confirms acceptance criteria coverage and user-scenario quality outcomes
- **Project Managers:** Coordinates test milestones, quality risks, and release readiness status

---

## Technical Leads/Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects guide architecture and implementation direction, ensuring technical decisions support reliability, scalability, and maintainability goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define and evolve architecture patterns and technical standards
- Lead technical trade-off decisions and design reviews
- Identify and manage technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Support implementation planning for complex or cross-team work
- Ensure non-functional requirements are addressed in design and delivery

### Goals
- Maintain long-term system quality and operability
- Reduce technical risk and unplanned rework
- Enable teams to deliver consistently within architectural guardrails

### Typical Communication
- Architecture/design review sessions
- Technical risk discussions during planning and execution
- Implementation guidance in design docs and code reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provides technical direction, reviews design choices, and unblocks complex implementation
- **Product Managers:** Translates product needs into feasible technical approaches and trade-offs
- **Project Managers:** Communicates technical dependencies, sequencing, and delivery risks

---

## Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives act as the business-facing voice for impacted teams, ensuring project decisions remain aligned with operational and cross-department needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate stakeholder requirements and constraints
- Validate that planned outcomes align with business priorities
- Surface organizational dependencies and adoption concerns
- Support prioritization and scope decisions with business context
- Participate in milestone reviews and feedback loops

### Goals
- Keep delivery aligned to stakeholder expectations and business impact
- Reduce downstream adoption friction and rework
- Improve decision quality through early stakeholder input

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder alignment meetings and milestone checkpoints
- Cross-functional requirement clarifications
- Feedback summaries and business-impact updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Shares operational context that affects implementation choices and rollout readiness
- **Product Managers:** Aligns requirements, priorities, and expected outcomes across business groups
- **Project Managers:** Coordinates stakeholder communications, approvals, and dependency tracking

---

## Sponsors/Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Sponsors/Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, authority, and resources for high-impact project decisions and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter, priorities, and major scope changes
- Allocate budget and organizational support
- Serve as escalation point for critical blockers and cross-org conflicts
- Review project health against strategic goals and outcomes
- Remove organizational barriers to execution

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable strategic value
- Enable timely executive decisions on trade-offs and risk
- Maintain momentum on high-priority initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Executive checkpoint reviews tied to milestones
- Escalation discussions for major blockers or trade-offs
- Outcome-focused updates with senior leadership

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provides top-level prioritization clarity when delivery trade-offs need executive direction
- **Product Managers:** Aligns product direction and investment decisions with strategic goals
- **Project Managers:** Receives status/risk escalations and supports resolution of organizational blockers

---

## Security/Compliance Officers

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure delivery practices and releases meet security, regulatory, and policy requirements throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for planning and execution
- Review design and implementation for security/control gaps
- Guide secure development, validation, and release readiness checks
- Track remediation of security findings and compliance actions
- Support incident response coordination and post-incident controls

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk in delivery
- Prevent avoidable vulnerabilities and control failures
- Establish repeatable security and compliance assurance practices

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews during planning and design
- Risk and remediation updates during execution
- Compliance sign-off and incident follow-up communications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Partners on secure implementation practices and remediation of findings
- **Product Managers:** Clarifies security/compliance constraints that influence scope and priority
- **Project Managers:** Tracks security/compliance dependencies, risks, and required approvals
